My Story

I'm Kaleigh

Founder · Mental Performance Consultant · Strength Coach

I've had a lifelong passion for sports and human performance. From my own experience as a collegiate athlete to working with athletes at every level, from youth development to professional baseball, I've seen how demanding sport can be. I've also learned that performance goes far beyond physical ability.

The most rewarding part of coaching has always been understanding the person behind the athlete: what motivates them, how they approach challenges, how they respond to adversity, and what helps them grow. That interest in people and the psychology behind performance is what led me to start Katalyst Performance.

Meaningful growth begins with self-awareness.

My approach helps athletes understand themselves, build practical mental and physical skills, gain confidence, and take ownership of their development, in sport and in life.

Kaleigh Edwards, MS, MS, CSCSFounder, Katalyst Performance
