Welcome to Katalyst Performance.

Katalyst is the spark that ignites growth.

The goal of Katalyst Performance is to help you recognize what's already within you and develop the tools to bring it out when it matters most. Through mental performance coaching, we'll work on building confidence, resilience, self-awareness, and the ability to navigate pressure with intention.

This process is about more than stats, playing time, or outcomes. It's about learning how you respond to challenges, how you prepare, what helps you perform at your best, and how you can take greater ownership of your performance.

That starts with understanding where you are right now.

This intake gives me a chance to get to know you as both an athlete and a person before we begin working together. There are no right or wrong answers. Be honest, take your time, and answer in a way that feels true to you.

06Confidentiality & Consent

Please read before submitting.

Before we get started, here's what's good to know. This is mental performance coaching — we're working on the mental side of your game together. It's not therapy or medical treatment, and if something comes up that would be better supported by a licensed therapist or doctor, I'll tell you, and I'll help you find the right person.

What you tell me stays between us. If you're under 18, I'll sometimes share general themes with a parent or guardian so they can support you at home — but the specifics of what we talk about are yours, not something I hand over, unless you want me to. The one exception: if I'm ever worried about your safety or someone else's, or a minor tells me about abuse or neglect, or the law requires it, I'll take the steps I need to keep you safe.

This is something you're choosing to do, and you can step away from it whenever you want. I can't promise a specific result — what you get out of this depends on both of us showing up for it. And since email and text aren't fully private, let's save anything sensitive for when we're actually talking.
